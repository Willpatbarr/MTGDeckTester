Services

What this is: every external service the app touches, why, and exactly when each one gets turned on.  What to do with it: before adding any new service, check the "Rejected as premature" table first — it is probably already on there.

One-sentence version

This is an offline-first local iOS app with no backend, so the service list is deliberately tiny — eight services, four of them not needed until a real tester or a real cost shows up.

Total cost, right now

$0. Every T0 service is free at this project's scale. Nothing here has a card on file.

Total cost after the T1 trigger

$99/yr. That is the Apple Developer Program membership, paid once the first tester who isn't you needs a build. Sentry stays free at T1; nothing else changes.

Trigger legend

T0 — needed to build or run the app at all, today, solo. T1 — needed the moment someone who isn't me touches the app or its data. T2 — only worth doing once a named, concrete threshold is actually crossed, not "eventually."
