Feature Inventory

What this is: every feature the app is committed to, grouped by when it ships.  What to do with it: use it as the scope reference for any "should we build X" question.

One-sentence version

MVP is a large, rules-accurate Commander sandbox with roughly 38 must-have features spanning decks, battlefield, full rules engine, AI, and history — everything else is explicitly Should-have, Someday, or out of scope.

Must-have — MVP must

Every row below is tagged and sourced. Almost all are [LOCKED] — they came directly from the reconciled specs, not from planning judgment. Groups roughly follow milestone delivery order; see milestones.html for exact scope per milestone.

Should-have — after MVP important

  • Deck editor
  • Deck legality validation (gap G5)
  • Commander damage (conflict C12)
  • Advanced search filters (colour, MV, type)
  • Battlefield annotations (arrows, highlights)
  • "Clean Up Board" auto-organize
  • Animated battlefield entry
  • Distinct face-down visual treatments
  • Battlefield search
  • Pinch-zoom on the play area (conflict C13 trigger)
  • Rules-aware zone-move validation
  • Rules-aware library search filtering
  • Position locking

Someday optional

  • Multiple bot rules modes (Simple / Assisted / Full — the S1 §9.4 idea, now inverted since Full is the baseline)
  • Online multiplayer
  • Matchmaking
  • Non-Commander formats
  • macOS/iPad-optimized layouts
  • Accessibility pass
  • Localization
  • AI difficulty beyond one strategy
  • Imperfect-information AI
  • Deck statistics and goldfish analytics

Explicitly out of scope, forever-until-stated

  • Full-rules coverage of every printed card (the engine is rules-accurate for what it implements; card coverage grows over time)
  • Anything server-side
  • Accounts
  • Monetization

What to do next

  1. Cross-check any new feature idea against the Should-have and Someday lists before adding it to a milestone.
